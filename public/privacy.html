Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2, 2026

Gameday ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains how the Gameday app handles your information.

Information We Collect

Gameday does not collect, store, or transmit any personal information. The app does not require you to create an account or sign in.

Data Stored on Your Device

The only data stored is your team preferences (which NBA and MLB teams you follow), saved locally on your device using your browser's local storage. This data never leaves your device.

Third-Party Services

Gameday displays publicly available sports scores and broadcast information. No personal data is shared with any third party.

Analytics

We do not use any analytics, tracking, or advertising services.

Children's Privacy

Gameday does not collect any personal information from anyone, including children under 13.
